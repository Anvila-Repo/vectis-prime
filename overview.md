# Overview

## Purpose
Technical founders often struggle to articulate their technological breakthroughs in terms that traditional and Web3 venture capitalists care about. They focus on how it works, while investors care about *who cares*, *how it scales*, and *how it captures value*.

Vectis Prime exists to close this gap. It acts as a continuous, high-intensity crucible that prepares software engineers and system architects to pitch, negotiate, and scale venture-backed enterprises in the highly competitive Fintech and Web3 domains.
